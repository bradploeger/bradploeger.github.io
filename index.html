Brad's Toolbox

Free tools that run entirely in your browser. No sign-up, no ads, nothing to install. Your work stays on your device.

Tools

  • Plotline Grapher

    A graphing calculator for functions, equations like x² + y² = 9, and elliptic curves over finite fields.

    • Sliders
    • Roots & intersections
    • Table of values
    • Mod p

  • NEXRAD Volume Viewer

    Turns a NEXRAD weather radar volume scan into a 3D model you can spin around. Pick any US radar and load the latest scan or one from the archive.

    • Points
    • Volume
    • Reflectivity shells
    • Velocity & dual-pol
